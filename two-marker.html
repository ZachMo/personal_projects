Two Marker Studio

Plan a coloring page with two markers and one light.

Working…

Drag a light's handle, or drag anywhere to move the selected light. The dotted border is outside the page, so a light there is out of frame.

What to lay down

Markers
Light markerwhere light falls
Shadow markerwhere light doesn't

These two markers are close in value, so light and shadow will blur together. Pick one much lighter than the other.

Lights
Shading

Small shapes cast shadows onto the larger shapes around them, pointing away from each light.

Page

Changing the line settings re-finds the shapes and clears painted regions. If color leaks between two shapes, close gaps or raise the cutoff.
